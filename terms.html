Academic support

Academic Ace provides consultation, tutoring, research-method guidance, and editing that supports student learning and preserves student authorship. The student remains responsible for research decisions, claims, analysis, citations, and submitted work. We do not complete assessed work for submission, fabricate sources or data, promise grades, or guarantee institutional approval.

Inquiry, estimate and quotation

A consultation inquiry is a request for scope review and is not a confirmed booking. Calculator amounts are indicative only. A personalised quotation states its currency, scope, amount, and any expiry date. A client response to a quotation records an intent to proceed; the engagement is confirmed only when the scope and any required written service terms are agreed by both parties.

Timing and communication

Turnaround depends on confirmed scope and availability. The team should agree on milestones and communication arrangements before work begins. An inquiry confirmation is not a promise of service availability.

Payments

No payment is processed by this website. An administrator may record a payment status after independently verifying it through the relevant financial institution or provider. The record is not proof that a payment processor has settled a transaction.
