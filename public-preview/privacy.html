Privacy notice

Last updated: 9 October 2026. This page describes a free informational preview. It is not a statement of legal compliance.

Owner action before public launch: add the legal business name and contact details, select and document an applicable retention period, confirm hosting and data-location arrangements, and obtain local privacy/legal review for the jurisdictions served.

Information collected

This preview has no inquiry form, account registration, file upload, or payment flow, and it does not store consultation details in an application database. The hosting provider may process standard request metadata to deliver and secure the site under its own terms. Do not submit personal, institutional, or academic information through this preview.

Purpose and access

The site provides general information about Academic Ace services. No administrator dashboard or inquiry records are available through this static preview.

Files, transport and storage

This preview does not accept file uploads. Do not send sensitive documents or credentials. The public pages are delivered over HTTPS by the hosting platform.

Retention and deletion

The application does not currently delete inquiry records automatically. The business owner must set a retention period, arrange a documented deletion process and ensure backup copies expire appropriately before collecting real client information at scale. To ask about access or deletion, use the contact method given to you by Academic Ace when replying to your inquiry; business contact details have not yet been supplied for publication.

Service providers

This static preview does not use the application database or a payment provider. Render hosts the published pages and may process standard request metadata.

Contact

Public business contact channels can be configured by the owner and are shown on the home page. A monitored privacy contact and legal business identity still need owner configuration before launch.
