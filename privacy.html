Privacy notice

Last updated: 9 October 2026. This notice describes the current consultation inquiry application. It is an operational notice, not a statement of legal compliance.

Owner action before public launch: add the legal business name and contact details, select and document an applicable retention period, confirm hosting and data-location arrangements, and obtain local privacy/legal review for the jurisdictions served.

Information collected

If you send an inquiry, Academic Ace stores the preferred name or alias, selected contact method and contact detail, project type, academic level, subject, institution if supplied, topic, citation/style information, workload, deadline and time zone, support requested, assessment criteria, and consent acknowledgements. Do not include passwords, student portal access, government identifiers, or information unrelated to scoping the request.

Purpose and access

The information is used to assess the requested consultation, prepare a scope and quotation, and contact you about that inquiry. Inquiry details are stored in the application database and are accessible to signed-in administrators. Access and significant administrative actions are recorded in an audit log. Public sample resources do not expose unpublished sample records.

Files, transport and storage

The current inquiry form does not accept or store files. If documents become necessary, the team should agree on a separate transfer method first. Production deployment must use HTTPS, secure hosting and suitable storage protections. The application itself does not encrypt the SQLite database file; the hosting operator must configure encrypted storage and backups. Do not send sensitive information until those controls and the retention policy have been reviewed.

Retention and deletion

The application does not currently delete inquiry records automatically. The business owner must set a retention period, arrange a documented deletion process and ensure backup copies expire appropriately before collecting real client information at scale. To ask about access or deletion, use the contact method given to you by Academic Ace when replying to your inquiry; business contact details have not yet been supplied for publication.

Service providers

The base application uses a local SQLite database and does not send inquiry data to an email, analytics, cloud file-storage or payment provider. A deployment may introduce hosting and backup providers; the operator must identify them in this notice before launch. No payment provider is connected.

Contact

Public business contact channels can be configured by the owner and are shown on the home page. A monitored privacy contact and legal business identity still need owner configuration before launch.
